03 / LIVE WEATHER & MARS24 MEDA / REMS

Real-time clocks & provenance

Tracks live Coordinated Mars Time (MTC), Mars Sol Date (MSD), and local solar time at Perseverance (Jezero) and Curiosity (Gale). Anchors local barometric pressure and 7-Sol temperature trends while clearly badging MEASURED vs. MODEL data.

Traceable NASA planetary datasets with transparent scientific provenance.

The mission planner streams live EPSG:4326 orbital tiles from NASA Mars Trek WMTS backed by a simulated terrain-model fallback (an approximation of MOLA-scale relief, not MOLA data) so the map never blanks out offline. Weather telemetry is anchored to Perseverance (MEDA) and Curiosity (REMS) Sol reports, while atmospheric scale-height interpolation, dust storm (SPE) simulations, and PLSS metabolic depletion rates are explicitly badged as physics models.
